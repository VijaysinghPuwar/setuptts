## SetupTTS v1.7.1

**Long jobs that finish.** Multi-hour audiobook exports now survive the computer going to sleep, Wi-Fi dropping out, and the Mac waking up with no connection — plus fixes for built-in macOS voices and a cleaner look in a few places.

---

### Long sessions

- **The computer stays awake while audio is being made.** On macOS and Windows (and Linux with systemd), SetupTTS holds off sleep for as long as a job is running and lets it go the moment the last job ends. The screen can still turn off. The Mac app also opts out of App Nap, so a job behind other windows isn't slowed down.
- **Connection lost? The job waits instead of failing.** When the internet drops — or the computer wakes from sleep and Wi-Fi is still reconnecting — the job shows *Waiting for internet connection…* and continues by itself as soon as Microsoft's service can be reached again (for up to 30 minutes). Before, a drop of more than about 30 seconds used up every retry and stopped the job.
- **Accurate chunk count from the start.** A 3-hour job no longer reads "Chunk 1 of ~4955" on its first, deliberately tiny, warm-up chunk.

### Built-in macOS voices

- **Text with `<` or tags is read in full.** macOS treated text like `5 < 6 > 4` or a stray `</voice>` as markup and silently skipped the rest of the chunk. Text containing `[[ ]]` is no longer run as a speech command (for example `[[slnc 5000]]` inserted five seconds of silence).
- **No duplicate voices after adding voices.** When an Enhanced voice is added in System Settings, macOS lists the voice twice and renames it ("Samantha (English (US))"). The list now shows each voice once, under its plain name, and a voice you had selected stays selected.

### Offline voices on Windows

- Offline voices now work when Windows has a user name with accents or other non-English letters (for example `C:\Users\José`) — the speech engine underneath could not open its files there. The portable EXE was most affected, since it unpacks into the user's folder.
- Removing an offline voice that is still in use shows a clear message instead of an error.

### Look and feel

- The voice download bar no longer has its percentage spilling out of it; progress is spelled out underneath ("Downloading Ryan (high) — 62% (76 of 121 MB)") and stays there while you search.
- Column headings in **Recent Conversions** and **Get Voices** line up with the columns under them.
- Long voice names end in "…" instead of being cut off mid-letter, and **Save To** shows the end of the folder path — the part that tells folders apart.

### Testing

- 488 automated tests, including network-outage and sleep/wake simulations and "chaos" runs of 6-hour books where about 1 request in 6 fails.
- A new `scripts/stress_voices.py` runs real jobs through every voice source — Microsoft online voices in 9 languages, offline Piper voices, and built-in macOS voices — with hostile text (markup, emoji, 3,000-character words, lists of numbers) and checks every MP3 it produces.

## SetupTTS v2.2.1

**Online voices no longer sound echoey: compression now defaults to Light.**

---

### Fixes

- **Online (Edge) voices sounded compressed and echoey.** v2.2.0 turned on audiobook compression at **Balanced** (32 kbit/s). Online audio already arrives as a 48 kbit/s MP3, so Balanced compresses it a second time, and at 32 kbit/s that second pass can be heard. The encoder drops the sample rate from 24 kHz to 22 kHz and cuts the treble, by 5 dB at 7–9 kHz and 12 dB at 9–11 kHz. Speech comes out muffled and swirly, which sounds like an echo.
- **The default is now Light (40 kbit/s).** It keeps the native 24 kHz, and every band up to 11 kHz stays within half a decibel of the original. Files are still 17% smaller for online voices and 38% smaller for offline voices.
- **Existing users are moved over automatically.** v2.2.0 saved its default into your settings, so if your setting still reads Balanced and you never changed it, it now becomes Light. Any level you pick in Settings from now on is kept, including Balanced.
- The Settings descriptions for **Balanced** and **Smallest** now say that online voices can sound muffled or echoey at those levels.

### Measured

These are real online voices (Ava, Andrew) run through the app's own job worker, compared with the uncompressed file. The distortion score is in-band log-spectral distance. Lower is better, and re-encoding at the same 48 kbit/s already scores about 3.

| Level | Sample rate | Distortion (Ava) | 7–9 kHz | 9–11 kHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Light** (new default) | 24 kHz | 3.4–3.7 | +0.4 dB | +0.1 dB |
| Balanced (old default) | 22 kHz | 5.5 | −4.9 dB | −11.9 dB |
| Smallest | 16 kHz | 13.2 | −33 dB | −37 dB |

### Tested

- 969 automated tests pass on macOS (6 are Windows-only and skipped).
- New tests check the following:
  - The default keeps 24 kHz and the speech bands.
  - An untouched v2.2.0 setting moves to Light.
  - Levels you chose yourself are never changed.
  - Saving Settings without touching the level doesn't count as choosing one.
- The tests were also run against the old default and with the migration removed, and they fail in both cases.
- A real 74-second online job came out at exactly 40 kbit/s and 24 kHz, 17% smaller, and macOS reports the correct duration.

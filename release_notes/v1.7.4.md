## SetupTTS v1.7.4

**Long jobs no longer slow down when SetupTTS is behind other windows.**

---

### Fixes

- **Jobs keep full speed while the window is hidden (macOS).** About a minute after SetupTTS went behind other windows, or the screen locked, macOS put it into App Nap, which lowers its CPU priority and slows its timers. An offline audiobook job then ran 3–4 times slower until the window came back to the front. For example, the same 375 000-character book took 59 minutes one day and 3 h 36 min the next, with nothing else changed. The setting earlier versions used to opt out of App Nap is ignored by current macOS. While a job runs, SetupTTS now tells macOS it is doing work the user asked for, and macOS stops throttling it. When the last job finishes, normal power saving comes back.
- **The same protection on Windows.** Windows 11 can also slow background apps to save power (shown as "Efficiency mode" in Task Manager). While a job runs, SetupTTS now opts out of this.

### Good to know

- Two jobs on the same offline voice share the computer's speed. Each shows roughly half the speed of one job alone, and both finish in about the same total time as running them one after the other.

### Tested

530 automated tests pass on macOS. On macOS 27 a hidden SetupTTS window was put into App Nap within about 70 seconds without the fix, and 1.7.3's Info.plist setting did not prevent it. With the fix the window stayed out of App Nap. The Windows opt-out is called for real in the Windows test run.

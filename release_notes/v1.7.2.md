## SetupTTS v1.7.2

**No more gibberish from offline voices, and no wrongly refused jobs.**

---

### Fixes

- **Offline voices no longer speak gibberish on long sentences.** Now and then, a voice (most often **Ryan (high)**) would turn a long sentence into made-up words for a few seconds — for example, *"…he still obtained part of your Divine Dao blood and soul!"* came out as nonsense every time. Long sentences are now spoken one clause at a time, split at commas, semicolons and colons, with a natural short pause. In testing with speech recognition, the two sentences that went wrong in a real audiobook were garbled 8 of 8 and 6 of 8 times before, and 0 of 8 after; 120 other sentences from the same book were unaffected.
- **A long job is no longer refused because of one hiccup at the start.** Before a long job, SetupTTS checks the voice with a short sample. A single "no audio" reply — common and harmless with Multilingual voices — stopped the whole job with *"The selected voice may not be compatible with this text"*. The check now tries up to three times and only turns a voice away if it keeps failing.

### Note on sleep

SetupTTS keeps the computer from going to sleep on its own while a job runs. **Closing a laptop's lid still puts it to sleep** (macOS always does this on battery). The job pauses, and when the computer wakes it waits for the internet to come back and continues where it left off.

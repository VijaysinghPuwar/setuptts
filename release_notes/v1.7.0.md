## SetupTTS v1.7.0

**Offline and built-in voices.** Alongside Microsoft's online neural voices, SetupTTS can now speak with voices that run entirely on your computer — no internet, account or setup needed.

---

### What's new

**Built-in voices from your computer**
- **Windows:** every voice installed on the PC is listed — Cortana, David, Zira, Mark and any language-pack or third-party SAPI voice. Both the classic "Desktop" voices and the newer voices Windows installs for Narrator are included.
- **macOS:** every voice from System Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Spoken Content.
- To add more, click **+ Get voices ▸ Add Windows voices…** (or **Add macOS voices…**), which opens the system page for installing voices. After installing, reopen SetupTTS (or click Retry) and they appear under **Built-in**.

**Free offline neural voices**
- One natural-sounding English voice (**Lessac**) comes with the installer and the macOS app, ready to use.
- **+ Get voices** lists about 170 more free voices in about 50 languages. Each one is a single download (most are 20–75 MB), checked for completeness before it is installed. You can cancel a download, and remove voices you no longer need.
- Offline voices run several times faster than real time on an ordinary laptop. On a test PC, a 1-hour audiobook took about 5 minutes.

**Redesigned voice picker**
- A new **All · Online · Offline · Built-in** switch above the search box.
- With **All** selected, the list is grouped under *Microsoft Online*, *Offline Neural* and *Built into this computer*. Each offline or built-in voice is tagged so you can see at a glance which voices need internet.
- **Preview** is now a compact ▶ button next to the voice list, which leaves more room for speed and export settings on small windows.
- **No internet?** SetupTTS now opens with the voices on your computer instead of an empty list, and tells you that the online voices will come back when you reconnect.

**Works the same as the online voices**
- Offline and built-in voices export ordinary MP3 files, use the speed slider, preview, run in the job list (two at a time), and show progress and time remaining.
- The MP3 is written under a temporary name and only moved into place when every part has been spoken. A cancelled or failed job never leaves a half-finished file, and never replaces a file you already had.

### Notes
- The **portable** Windows EXE does not include the bundled offline voice, because it unpacks everything on every launch. Built-in voices work immediately, and offline voices can be downloaded from **+ Get voices**.
- Chinese, Japanese, Hebrew and Thai offline voices are not offered yet. They need extra language tools that are not included. The online voices cover these languages.
- Offline voices don't report their gender in the catalog. Voices with unknown gender show under **All**, not under Female or Male.

### Under the hood
- New offline voice engine built on Piper and ONNX Runtime. Built-in Windows voices run through a small PowerShell helper process, so a misbehaving third-party voice can't crash SetupTTS. If the helper dies mid-job, it is restarted and the part is retried.
- 68 new automated tests (450 in total), and the packaged-app self-test in CI now checks that the bundled offline voice and the system voices actually speak inside the built installer, DMG and zip.
- Stress-tested with multi-hour books on both engines (an 11-hour book on a built-in voice in under 6 minutes), five jobs plus previews at once, 30 random cancels, the voice helper being killed mid-job, and hostile text (emoji, mixed scripts, XML-like tags, 5,000-letter words).
- Offline voices keep memory in check: long unpunctuated passages (number lists, pasted tables) are split before they reach the voice model, and jobs sharing a voice take turns. Memory stays around 0.5–0.9 GB even with several jobs and a preview at once.

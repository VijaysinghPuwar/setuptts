## SetupTTS v1.8.1

**Cleaner logs for offline voices.**

---

### Fixes

- **No more "Missing phoneme" warnings flooding the log.** For words like "button" or "little", the pronunciation step marks the last consonant with a special syllable sign that some offline voices, including Ryan (high), have no sound for. Piper already skipped that sign, but it logged a warning every time. One 100-chapter book produced 290 of them, which buried real warnings. SetupTTS now leaves the sign out before the voice sees it, and notes it once per voice.
- **The audio is exactly the same.** The voice receives the same input as before: on 150 000 characters of a real book (3 311 sentence pieces) with Ryan (high), the model input was identical with and without the change, and the warnings went from 70 to 0.

### Unchanged

- Offline voice speed on Mac is the same. On a MacBook Pro M5 with Ryan (high), this version averaged 209 characters per second and 1.8.0 averaged 204 over alternating runs, which is within normal variation.
- Windows GPU support (beta, from 1.8.0) is unchanged.

### Tested

749 automated tests pass on macOS. They include new tests showing that the voice gets identical input, that no Piper warning is logged, that each unknown sign is noted only once, and that two threads using a voice for the first time at once are safe.
